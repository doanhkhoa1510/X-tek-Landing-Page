## Role Responsibilities

- Assessing current state, identifying customer requirements, and defining the future state and/or business solution.
- Understanding as-is process, analyzing users' requirements and documents requirements.
- Develops and documents workflow, systems requirements, system impact analysis, systems design, process analysis and testing using Agile development methodology.
- Perform interviews with Business Users / SMEs.
- Elaborate and update Product Backlogs, User Story requirements.
- Act as a liaison between the development team and product owners, ensuring clear communication of project goals, requirements, and progress.

## Requirements/Qualifications

- Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Engineering, or related field.
- 5+ years of relevant experience in IT industry and at least 2 years in BA Lead role.
- Experience with Requirement management tools such as Jira, MS office, Azure DevOps
- Strong analytical and proven ability to quickly understand complex business and systems.
- Excellent interpersonal, English oral & writing communication skills and ability to effectively collaborate with diverse people such as clients, developers, quality assurances, subject matter experts, and architects.
- Have Figma design experience is a plus.
- Have experience as a Scrum Master is a plus.
