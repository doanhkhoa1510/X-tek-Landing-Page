## Role Responsibilities

- Participate in architecture design with scalability, performance, security, easy maintenance and create development environments along with Client's Architects.
- Transform the architectural design of the application into framework.
- Provide in-depth technical and hands-on leadership and guidance to the development & support team, ensuring alignment with project goals and objectives.
- Lead code reviews, design discussions, and technical planning sessions to drive continuous improvement.
- Manage the development process to ensure that design, coding standards are being followed, application requirements are fully implemented, technologies are efficiently and correctly implemented, and a high level of maintainability and extensibility is achieved.
- Support for development, bug fixings. Respond to user-reported issues, troubleshoot technical problems, and provide timely resolutions.
- Investigate and diagnose software defects, identifying root causes and implementing effective fixes.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including designers, product managers, and other developers, to deliver high-quality technical solutions.

## Requirements/Qualifications

- Bachelor’s degree in computer science, engineering, or related field.
- 7+ years of relevant experience in IT industry and at least 2 years in Technical Architect role.
- Solid technical background with good knowledge in OOP & SOA principles, design patterns, industry best practices for software development following Agile methodology.
- Proven experience in a Technical Architect role for mobile application and enterprise. application, with demonstrated ability to guide and mentor a development team and handle day-to-day activities.
- Strong experience in React, .NET framework, Azure Application Services, Azure Integration Services, Azure SQL development to develop enterprise applications.
- Experience in Azure LLM, LangGraph is a plus.
- Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills.
- Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.
- Excellent interpersonal, English oral & writing communication skills and ability to effectively. collaborate with diverse people such as clients, developers, quality assurances, subject matter experts, and architects.
