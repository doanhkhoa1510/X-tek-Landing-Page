# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro + Three.js (user choice). The 3D scene is a client-side island; the rest of the page is static HTML.

## Users

B2B decision makers: CTOs, founders, and product owners. They are looking for an engineering partner to build, modernize, or staff a digital product. They compare vendors quickly and look for proof of delivery, location, and time-zone coverage.

## Product Purpose

XionTechs builds consumer-centric software for other businesses. The landing page makes a visitor start a conversation ("Talk with us" / contact) with the team.

## Positioning

"Xion" is Mount Zion, the legendary mountain of Jerusalem. XionTechs is the team that takes a product from base camp to summit, with the tagline "Get IT Done". It is US-fronted (Dallas, Texas) with Vietnam delivery centers (Ho Chi Minh City, Da Nang). This gives US clients a US contact point and continuous build capacity across both time zones.

## Operating Context

Services (from xiontechs.com):
1. Product Engineering
2. AI-Driven Analytics
3. Product Co-Creation
4. Staffing Services
5. Custom App Development and legacy modernization
6. Low-code Solutions (Power Platform, Make, n8n, Retool, Bubble)
7. AI-Powered Solutions
8. Cloud Computing (Azure, AWS, GCP)

Process "4-D": Discovery → Design → Development → Deployment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The hero must use Three.js 3D. The chosen reference is unitedcarriers.com (a dark particle WebGL hero with a rim glow and route arcs). In place of the globe, the 3D shape is a **mountain**.
- The page must have a UI element that points to office locations. **Texas (Dallas) and Ho Chi Minh City** are required. Da Nang is the third office.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: XionTechs. Tagline: "Get IT Done". Secondary line: "Tech Made Simple, Solutions Made Powerful".
- Logo colors: navy `#103060` and amber `#F8C000`. The mark is an "X" with an amber swoosh.
- The mountain metaphor is binding: Xion = Mount Zion, the legendary mountain. The 3D mountain is its real relief.
- Pronunciation: XionTechs is said /siː.ɒn.teks/ ("see-on-teks"). Show it wherever the name needs teaching.

## Evidence on Hand

- Stats: 25+ years of leadership experience, 3 strategic office locations, 20+ tech stacks.
- Offices: Dallas, USA; Ho Chi Minh City (Viettel Tower, Floor 12, 285 CMT8); Da Nang (Floor 15, B1 Building, 279 Nguyen Tri Phuong).
- Contact: +1 469 322 9899 / +84 908 107552.
- Case study titles only: Cloud Security Service, Data Analytics System, IT Solution & Consultancy. The user will supply full case studies later.
- Testimonials: the current site's testimonials are template lorem copy ("Monotonectally harness…"). They are **not usable**. They ship as marked placeholders until the user supplies real ones.
- Absent, never fabricate: client logos, metrics per case, pricing, certifications.

## Product Principles

1. Show the climb: every section moves the visitor up the mountain, from problem to shipped product.
2. Two continents, one team: always show where the people are.
3. Proof over adjectives: stats and real cases, never filler praise.
4. One action: start a project conversation.
